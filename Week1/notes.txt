In Part B, I used the `//` operator to get the quotient and the `%` operator to get the remainder. In `digits.py`, these operators help extract individual digits from a number. In `timeconv.py`, they help convert seconds into hours, minutes, and seconds. In `change.py`, they help calculate the number of currency notes and the remaining amount.
For example, while writing `digits.py`, I initially used `n // 10` to extract the last digit, but it gave me the number without its last digit. I corrected it by using `n % 10`, which gives the last digit. This helped me understand how quotient and remainder work together.
